Cheque Management System

Desktop app · Zahrh Esther Hardware Trading LLC, Sharjah, UAE · in daily use since October 2026

A Windows app to write, print, save and track supplier cheques for a hardware trading company. The finance team uses it with real cheque books, real suppliers and real printed cheques, on several office computers sharing one database.

  • Printing that matches the paper. A cheque layout designer with per-bank offsets (verified on SIB and RAK Bank cheques), range printing, and amounts converted to words.
  • The whole cheque life cycle: pending, printed, cleared, bounced, stopped, cancelled and spoiled, plus security cheques, received customer cheques, supplier bills and balances, payment vouchers, a calendar view and reports.
  • Built for an office, not a demo: user roles (Admin and Staff), auto-lock, a full change log, password-protected daily backups, and an offline mode that saves and prints cheques without internet and syncs them later.
  • Engineering discipline: a pnpm monorepo with pure business rules in their own package (100% test coverage enforced for money, words and dates), database migrations tested in PGlite against every role and function, Row-Level Security in Supabase, and a hardened Electron setup (context isolation, sandbox, no secret keys in the app).

Electron React TypeScript Supabase / PostgreSQL Row-Level Security Vitest PGlite Windows installer

Pakistan Plants: billing for nurseries

Mobile app · iOS and Android · used day to day by businesses in Lahore

A billing and credit-tracking app made for Pakistani plant nurseries, fully bilingual in English and Urdu (Noto Nastaliq, right-to-left layout).

  • Invoices in seconds from a catalogue of 280+ plants with English and Urdu names, eight bill templates, PDF sharing straight to WhatsApp and automatic invoice numbering.
  • Udhaar (credit) tracking per customer, partial and advance payments, overdue alerts and ageing buckets (0–30, 31–60, 60+ days).
  • Reports by date, client and plant, exported to PDF or CSV; a customer database with contact import and one-tap call or WhatsApp.
  • Store listings, privacy policy, terms and a testing checklist prepared for release.

React Native / Expo TypeScript Supabase i18n (EN / UR, RTL) PDF generation EAS

DimSum POS: a restaurant point-of-sale

Desktop app · for my family’s restaurant in Lahore

An offline-first Windows point-of-sale system, designed from how the restaurant actually runs: I also manage its staff, suppliers and marketing.

  • Specified before it was coded: a software requirements specification, an entity-relationship design, the architecture, 16 user flows, a screen and permission matrix and wireframes, then milestones.
  • Orders for dine-in, takeaway, phone delivery and Foodpanda (with channel pricing); kitchen tickets and customer receipts on a thermal printer.
  • Controls: PIN login for owner and cashiers, discounts and voids that need the owner’s PIN, refunds with reason codes, inventory with low-stock alerts, expenses, and shift closing with cash reconciliation.

Electron React TypeScript Drizzle ORM Thermal printing Turborepo

EyeQ: eye tracking for learning

Final-year project · BSc Computer Science, Bahria University

A cross-platform mobile app that tracks students’ eye movements with computer vision to estimate how difficult a quiz question is and how attentive the learner is. The capture and analysis code was written and optimised for real-time feedback, with TensorFlow and PyTorch models and Firebase for data, and the app was tested with 20 students.

Python TensorFlow PyTorch OpenCV React Native Firebase
